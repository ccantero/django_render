Passkeys and account security

Passkeys let you sign in with your device screen lock, biometrics, or a security key. Your private key stays with your authenticator.

Keep your password available as a recovery method and register a second passkey when possible. If a device is lost, sign in with your password or another registered passkey, revoke the lost passkey here, and register a replacement. If you cannot sign in, contact the dashboard operator through the established account-recovery process; email access alone does not reset authentication.
